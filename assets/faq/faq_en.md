# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## What is UniLost & Found?
UniLost & Found is the official platform for managing lost and found items within the university community. Our goal is to quickly and securely connect students who have lost a belonging with those who have found it.

## How do I report an item I found?
1. Go to the **Home** tab and press the **I found something** button.
2. Take a clear photo of the item (highly recommended).
3. Add a descriptive title and select the appropriate category.
4. Indicate the location on campus and the date.
5. Click on **Publish ad**.

## How do I report an item I lost?
1. Go to the **Home** tab and press the **I lost something** button.
2. Fill in the details of the lost item.
3. The system will automatically search for potential matches in the database to suggest similar findings.

## Is it safe to contact other users?
Yes, all communication takes place through a secure internal chat integrated into the application. To start a chat, it is mandatory to have verified your institutional UAB email address (@uab.cat).

## How does the proximity filter work?
When selecting the **Proximity** option at the top of the item feed, the app will ask you for location permissions. If granted, the feed will automatically sort showing the closest items to your current GPS position within the university premises first.

## What should I do if I recover my item?
If you are the post creator, you can go to **My activity** in your profile, select the post and mark it as returned or delete it to keep the system updated.
