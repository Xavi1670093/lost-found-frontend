# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected, processed, stored, and protected when using UniLost & Found, in compliance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and local data protection regulations (such as LOPDGDD).

## 1. Data Controller
The processing of personal data collected through this application is carried out with the sole purpose of managing communication between users for the return of lost items within the university environment.

## 2. Data We Collect
In order to provide our service, we collect only the necessary and relevant data:
- **Registration Data:** First name, last name, institutional (university) email address, and unique user identifier.
- **Item Data:** Title, description, category, approximate location of the find, and photographs of the item that you choose to publish.
- **Account Configuration:** Language preferences, visual mode, and notification consents.

## 3. Purpose of Processing
Your personal data is used for the following purposes:
- To identify and authenticate registered users within the community.
- To facilitate the publication of advertisements for lost or found items.
- To allow secure and direct communication between the user who lost an item and the user who found it.
- To send service notifications essential for the operation of the application.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing
The legal basis for processing your data is the **explicit consent** granted by the user when accepting this Privacy Policy and registering on the platform, as well as the necessity of processing for the execution of the requested service.

## 5. Recipients and Data Transfer
- **No transfer to third parties:** We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party companies or organizations outside the university or the service.
- **Cloud Security:** Data is stored securely using Firebase infrastructure.

## 6. User Rights (GDPR)
Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:
- **Access:** The right to know what data we process.
- **Rectification:** The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** The right to request the deletion of your data from the platform.
- **Restriction and Objection:** The right to object to processing or request restriction of processing.
- You can exercise these rights directly through the configuration options in your profile within the application or by contacting administrative support.
